Greatest Films

A crime thriller set in Boston about an undercover cop and a mole in the police force trying to uncover eachother's identities. Stars an extremely talented cast including Leonardo DiCaprio, Matt Damon, Jack Nicholson, Mark Wahlberg and Martin Sheen. Won the Academy Award for Best Picture in 2007, and also happens to be my favorite movie.

A crime thriller/drama following the life and true story of Henry Hill. The film displays his rise and fall as a new York ganster, showing the consequences of organized crime. Stars Robert De Niro, Ray Liotta, and Joe Pesci and is widely regarded as one of the greatest films of all time.

A biographical drama following the early life of billionaire and film producer Howard hughes (played by Leonardo DiCaprio) from the 1920s to the 1940s. The movie explores his rise to fame in hollywood and his dedication to aviation and aircraft design. Won the 2005 academy Awards for Best Cinematography and Best Production Design.

A sports drama inspired by the life of boxing champion Jake LaMotta. The film explores his rise in boxing and his tragic unraveling outside of it, portraying how his unrelenting rage fueled his success as a fighter but ultimately consumed his personal life and relationships.
